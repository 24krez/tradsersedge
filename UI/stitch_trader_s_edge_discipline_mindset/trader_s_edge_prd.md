# Trader's Edge - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Overview
Trader's Edge is a mindset-focused mobile app delivering short, powerful trading reminders to a trader's lock screen. It acts as a professional mentor to reinforce discipline, manage emotions, and protect capital.

## Vision
Focus on the person reading the chart, not just the charts.
- Stay disciplined
- Avoid revenge trading
- Follow risk management
- Think in probabilities
- Protect capital
- Trade with confidence

## Design Direction
- **Theme:** Luxury Trading Terminal (James Bond / Bloomberg Terminal / Modern Wealth Apps)
- **Colors:** Deep Navy Blue (Primary), Gold (Secondary), White (Accent), Near Black (Background).
- **Typography:** Modern, Minimal, Professional.
- **Vibe:** Calm, professional, high-stakes but disciplined.

## Core Features
1. **Lock Screen Mindset Notifications:** Session-based reminders (NY, London, Asia).
2. **Trading Personality Modes:** Agent (James Bond), Coach, Drill Sergeant.
3. **Notification Categories:** Discipline, Risk Management, Confidence, Emotional Control, Funded Trader.
4. **Daily Mission Briefing & EOD Debrief.**
5. **Streak System:** Tracking discipline consistency.

## Planned Screens
1. **Welcome/Onboarding:** "Welcome Agent. Your edge is discipline."
2. **Session Selection:** NY, London, Asia toggles.
3. **Personality Choice:** Mode selection with personality descriptions.
4. **Category Settings:** Fine-tuning reminder types.
5. **Home Dashboard:** Today's Mission, Streak, Next Reminder, Recent Messages.
